# নদী — a real-time 3D Bengal khal at golden hour, up through Bara Moheshkhali to a shapla bil

Standalone site: Vite 8 + TypeScript 7 + three r186. Almost everything (terrain, trees, grass, huts, boats, most textures, sound) is generated in code. Two exceptions: six CC0 Poly Haven textures (colour + normal, 1K WebP, ≈4.5 MB) in `public/textures/` for the ground layers and the bark (chosen by the user in the second session), and the people, built as game characters are from MakeHuman's CC0 body, skeleton, skins, eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes and hair (`public/humans/`, ≈1.3 MB, made by `scripts/humans/`). There are no audio files.

## Start here

The user chose the world "the khal and the bil only" and the features "journey + camera + film only" (no map, voyages, walking, helm or GIS mode). `HANDOFF.md` §1 and §4 have the request and what it changed.

## Commands

- `npm run dev` serves on http://localhost:5173 (on `localhost` only: pass `--base http://localhost:5173/` to the headless scripts, whose default is 127.0.0.1). `npm run build` writes `dist/` (hashed files in `dist/bundle/`). `npm run typecheck` runs `tsc --noEmit`. `npm run preview` serves `dist/`.
- Visual check (only when asked): with the dev server running, `node scripts/headless/shoot.mjs <outDir> --base http://localhost:5173/ --shots '<json>'`. It saves PNGs and prints draw calls, triangles, frame ms and every page console message. It needs Chrome (set `CHROME_PATH` if Chrome is not at the default Windows path). The shot format and the debug API are documented in `HANDOFF.md`.
- Geo data (`GEOGRAPHY_PLAN.md`; `scripts/geo/`):
  - `node scripts/geo/fetch.mjs [ids…] [--force]` downloads the open data into `scripts/geo/cache/` (≈320 MB, git-ignored). Sources and licences are in `scripts/geo/config.mjs`; each file's checksum and date go into `cache/manifest.json`. Some hosts need the User-Agent (`lib/http.mjs`); big COGs are read in strips (`lib/raster.mjs`); tiles inside multi-GB zips are taken by range requests (`lib/zip.mjs`).
  - `node scripts/geo/tide-fit.mjs <years…>` fits the Cox's Bazar tide harmonics (`src/geo/tide.ts`, re-exported by `scripts/geo/lib/tide.mjs`). The bake still uses the tide to sort the sea's ground (`tidal`); the scene has no tide.
  - `node scripts/geo/audit.mjs` prints the P0 audit and writes satellite crops to `cache/audit/`. The findings are in `GEO_AUDIT.md`.
  - `node scripts/geo/bake.mjs` writes `public/geo/` for the window `CROP` (`src/geo/project.ts`): terrain (dm, 30 m ground grid), kind, level (inland water's surface), cover, canopy, OSM lines, `buildings.bin` (OSM's footprints and Microsoft's where OSM has none: a ninth of the ordinary ones, every named or tagged one, at 1:3 places and real size, clear of each other; names in `buildings.json`), `places.json`, `manifest.json`. Layers are deflate-raw (decompress with `DecompressionStream('deflate-raw')`). Its Matarbari parts (`ASH_PONDS`, `PORT_ZONE`) lie outside this window and do nothing here. Landmarks and what ground they must stand on are in `scripts/geo/places.mjs` (here: Bara and Chhota Moheshkhali, the old bridge, the shapla bil).
  - The bake's kinds: water and flats joined to the sea, and from them the bare ground (WorldCover bare, water, wetland) below the spring tides' high water, are the sea's (`tidal`); fields behind their bunds stay land.
  - `node scripts/geo/check.mjs` checks the bake against OSM's coastline and the landmarks, and draws OSM's lines over the bake and Sentinel-2 in `cache/view/` (and a crop round the khal, `khal-*.png`).
- `?quality=low|high` holds the quality tier (without it, `main.ts` drops to `low` by itself when even the adaptive resolution's floor runs under 30 fps).
- People (`scripts/humans/`, all MakeHuman, CC0): `node scripts/humans/fetch.mjs` downloads the base mesh, the default skeleton and weights and the macro targets into `scripts/humans/cache/` (git-ignored), and from the 280 MB system asset pack only the members `config.mjs` lists (`PACK_FOLDERS`), by range requests (`scripts/geo/lib/zip.mjs`). `node scripts/humans/build.mjs` builds the characters of `config.mjs` (`CHARACTERS`: `majhi`, `meye`; macro sliders, height, skins, eyes, brows, lashes, hair, `wear`) into `public/humans/humans.json` + `humans.bin` (deflate-raw; arrays shared between characters are stored once) and writes `cache/textures.json`; `python scripts/humans/textures.py` (Python 3 + Pillow) makes the WebP textures (the skin a blend of a light and a dark MakeHuman skin evened to the character's tone, with stubble and a moustache from the beard mask build.mjs rasterises in the body's UV layout; hair, brows and lashes recoloured with their colour bled under the transparent texels; the iris darkened). Never hand-edit `public/humans/`.
- The build warns that a chunk is over 500 kB. That is expected: three.js is a large share of it.

## Architecture (`src/`)

- `geo/project.ts`: the lat/lon ↔ world projection (`MAP_SCALE = 3`, origin at Gorakghata jetty's landward end, kept from the larger world) and `CROP` (the baked window round Bara Moheshkhali, ≈1.3 × 1.1 km of world). No warp: world metres are real positions on the plain 1:3 map. `toGround`/`fromGround`/`toWorld` (the scripts use them). `geo/data.ts`: loads the baked map (`public/geo/`) with top-level await before anything is built, so geography stays synchronous: `heightAt`, `kindAt` (`KIND`), `coverAt` (`COVER`), `canopyAt`, `inlandSurfaceAt`, `cellAt`/`cellCentre`, `GRID_W`/`GRID_H`/`CELL` (10 m world cells), `LAYERS` (the raw grids), `PLACES`/`place(id)`, `LINES` (OSM, world metres), `BUILDINGS` (centre, length, width, yaw, `BUILDING` kind, storeys, important), `WORLD` (the baked extent), `ATTRIBUTION`. On failure it shows a Bangla error in `#err`.
- `core/globals.ts`: `G`, the uniforms every scene shader shares (time, pass camera position, sun, key light, glow, fog, mist, night, wind and gust phase, `uWaterLevel` and `uTideHigh` (held at the khal's level, `main.ts`), which water stands where (`tLevel`/`uLevelMap`, set by `buildWater`), the mirror's level (`uMirrorLevel`), the khal's current against its mean (`uFlowScale`, the season's runoff), the season's `uSeasonA`/`uSeasonB`/`uCrops`, wet, rain, overcast). Materials hold references to these objects, so mutate `.value` and never replace an object. Layers: `LAYER_WATER = 1`, `LAYER_NO_REFLECT = 2`. Both are drawn by the main camera only; the mirror camera sees layer 0.
- `render/glsl.ts`: `GLSL_COMMON` (uniform declarations, hash/noise/fbm, `windGust`, `plotFrame`/`PLOT_SIZE`/`cropShares` (the paddy plots and what each holds now), `waterLevels`/`waterSurface` (which water stands over a point and its surface; none on dry ground), `hazeColor`, `applyAtmosphere` (height fog and mist, and past the map's edges the land fading into the haze, `EDGE_HAZE`)). Include it exactly once per shader. Also `CROP_REACH` (how far the 3D crops stand; inside it the terrain paints the ground under them).
- `render/patch.ts`: `patchMaterial(material, options)` injects the shared atmosphere into built-in materials, plus optional wind sway, grime, translucency, terrain paddy plots, riverbed caustics, foliage normals, `skin`, and `splat` (the photo ground layers), through `onBeforeCompile`; also `seasonal: 'leaf' | 'tree' | 'kash'` (the season's tint, spring blossom on a share of the trees, kash plumes only from late in the rains through autumn) and `vertex` hooks. The terrain's `aZone` is signed: paddy plots > 0 (< 0 was the salt pans: that shader branch is inert here); still water standing on the ground mirrors the sky (`atmBrine`). It sets `material.defines` and `customProgramCacheKey`; any new option must change that key. Its fog step also calls `underwater()` (in `glsl.ts`): anything below the water level fades into `G.uWaterBody` with the length of water it is seen through.
- `render/surfaces.ts`: loads the photo textures (`GROUND_LAYERS` grass, dirt, mud, sand, pebbles as two `DataArrayTexture`s, colour and normal, plus each layer's mean colour; and the bark). A set that fails to load is `null`, and the scene falls back to its painted look.
- `render/pipeline.ts`: `Pipeline` runs, in order: mirror pass (half-res `PlanarReflection`, `uReflPass = 1`, `uCamPos` = mirror camera) → scene (HalfFloat, MSAA ×4) → bright pass → 5-level bloom → radial light shafts → composite (ACES, grade, vignette, grain, chromatic fringe, fades, letterbox) straight to the canvas.
- `render/reflection.ts`: Reflector-style mirror camera for the plane at `G.uMirrorLevel` (the level of the water nearest the eye, chosen each frame by `water.update`), with an oblique near plane.
- `render/builder.ts`: `MeshBuilder` (merges procedural parts into one geometry), `rgb()` (sRGB hex → linear triple), `taperedTube`, `card`, `slab` (a box with metre UVs, turned by a yaw). `render/instancing.ts`: `instanced()` splits placements into slabs of 600 m along z by 1300 m across x (`chunk`, `across`) so off-screen slabs are culled. `render/cull.ts`: `cullBeyond(root, far)` records each mesh's world bounding sphere once; `updateCulling(eye)` (each frame, before drawing) leaves meshes undrawn beyond `far` (× `setCullReach`, 0.7 in the low tier) (`main.ts`: buildings 1800 m, vegetation 1900 m, roads 2600 m). `render/textures.ts`: every texture is painted on a canvas and turned into a `DataTexture`. Rows are flipped so v = 1 is the canvas top, and `bleed` fills transparent texels with the average colour.
- `world/khal.ts`: the khal the boat goes up, the frame the world is laid out round. Its middle is OSM's canal through Bara Moheshkhali (the line nearest the old bridge), smoothed, from `START_BELOW` m below the bridge up to its head at the hills' feet and on along a curve into the bil (`INTO_BIL` m past its middle); points every `STEP` (2 m). `JOURNEY` (`length` ≈1226 m, `bridge` ≈156, `head` ≈962: metres along it), `khalAt(s)` (point and heading), `khalFrame(x, z)` (within 60 m: `s` along it, `d` from its middle, `side` +1 left going up; a reused object), `khalHalfWidth(s)` (7–11 m, 6.2 under the bridge, 15 into the bil: its real width, [মডেল]), `khalDepth(s)`, `KHAL_LEVEL` (one water level for the khal and the bil, from the bridge's ground), `KHAL_CURRENT` (0.07 m/s down toward its sluice, × `uFlowScale`). The real ground rises ≈4 m along it, so the strip round it is let down (or built up below the bridge) to lie `PLAIN_ABOVE` over its water, easing back to the map's own ground between `FULL` and `NONE` m from it: `corridorDrop`, `khalDistance`, `baseGround(x, z)` (the map's ground so brought down, before anything is cut in). `BIL` (the shapla bil at the khal's head, `place('shapla-bil')`, its long axis the way the khal comes in, at `KHAL_LEVEL`), `bilLocal`/`bilPoint`, `bilReach`.
- `world/geography.ts` is the single source of truth for land and water: `terrainHeight` (`baseGround` with the roads' embankments (`roadGround`) and the ponds' banks (`pondBank`), the khal cut in (bed shoaling to the sides, a mud bank `BANK` m wide up to a low levee `LEVEE` above the water), the bil's bowl and OSM's small khals, streams and drains), `waterLevelAt` (the khal's and the bil's `KHAL_LEVEL`, a pond's or a small khal's own; −∞ on dry ground), `shoreLevel` (the level of the water whose bank a point is on: the wet band reads it), `khalBank` (0..1 on the khal's mud slope and levee strip: plots keep off it), `inlandWaterAt`, `nearBackwater`, `mangroveZone`; the bil's `bilMarsh`, `bilDepth`, `routeGap` (distance from the boat's way); `BRIDGE` (at `khalAt(JOURNEY.bridge)`, its frame `bridgeFrame`/`bridgeToWorld`, `bridgeSoffit`, `bridgeDeck`, `onBridge`, `bridgePath`; heights above the khal's water `BRIDGE.water`); the small khals (`REACHES` from OSM's waterways, widths by kind at 1:3 [মডেল], water `FREEBOARD` below the land; `eachWaterwayPoint` paints the khal and them into the water's level map); `PONDS` (the map's ponds, one per 60 m, each with its `level` let down with the ground; none on the khal's own course); `WETLANDS` (none); `onLandmark` (the bridge). The world is the baked area (`WORLD_X0..X1`, `WORLD_Z1..Z0`): x west, z north, heights real metres about MSL. All the water is inland; there is no sea.
- `world/roads.ts`: OSM's roads at real width on the 1:3 map, by highway tag (`ROAD_KINDS`: width, how far the crest stands above the land, surface: bitumen, herringbone brick or earth [মডেল]); a road beside the khal is moved out onto its levee (`besideTheKhal`: the khal is drawn wider than at 1:3), and a short run from one bank to the other is a crossing on a deck; crests from `baseGround` smoothed along each road; `roadGround` levels the ground to them (shoulders easing to the land), `roadCover` and `onRoad` for painting and placements; `clearOfRoads`; `buildRoads()` draws the surfaces from one texture atlas (`roadAtlas` in `render/textures.ts`), one mesh per 1300 m cell, and bridges as decks with parapets. At the old bridge the arch carries the path; the bil has no roads. `core/segmentIndex.ts`: `SegmentIndex`, segments filed in square cells for the nearest one measured against its half-width (the khal, the small khals and the roads).
- `world/terrain.ts`: `Terrain`, the ground as quadtree patches round the camera (64 × 64 quads each, leaves 128 m, split nearer than 1.5 sizes, skirts wound outward, a few ms of building a frame with the nearest built ancestor standing in, `update(eye, true)` builds all for stills); colour and layer weights per vertex from the map's cover (`LOOK` per `COVER` class, blended over the four cells round a point), the beds under water, the wet mud up a bank from `shoreLevel`. It also exports `groundAt(x, z)` / `Cover`: painted colour, field zone (signed) and the photo-layer weights at a point; `groundLattice` for the chunked builders. The terrain's `aSplat` attribute and the meadow's grass density both come from it.
- `world/crops.ts`: 3D crops in the paddy plots round the camera (out to `CROP_REACH`): rice in rows (seedling, growing, ripe with nodding panicles, stubble) and winter mustard in flower, in three LODs (real plants < 20 m, row cards + canopy < 44 m, canopy only beyond). Each patch works out its plot's crop in the vertex shader from `plotFrame`/`cropShares`, so the year turning regrows the fields with nothing rebuilt; rice and mustard meshes share instance buffers (the absent one is collapsed below ground); the wind bends each plant from its foot (`sway`). Patches are laid per plot between its bunds, off roads and paths.
- `world/meadow.ts`: `Meadow(huts, ghats)`: dense blade grass and wild flowers round the camera, built in 12 m chunks (two levels of detail, a few chunks per frame, from a 1.5 m height lattice) into one `InstancedMesh` per layer; none in the water or on the mud just above it, on roads and paths, on the bridge or round the ghats. `world/drift.ts`: water hyacinth as position-based dynamics: 720 plants (each its own body, tied to neighbours in mats by tension-only root links that snap when overstretched) and 160 leaves, on the still fresh water round the viewer (`REACH`, `inlandWaterAt`, at least `FLOATS` deep): in the khal (and on its way across the bil) set down only within `EDGE` (3 m) of the banks, its middle being the boats' way; at their water's own level on its ripples, drifting a little downwind and with the khal's current, colliding through a spatial hash, pushed out of the hero hull and by the paddle's blade, nudged off the banks; recycled round the viewer. `world/ponds.ts`: `LILY_PONDS` (about a third of the ponds, [মডেল]) and their lily pads and flowers (the wetlands' reeds and hyacinth too, with none to place here).
- `world/water.ts`: two meshes on one polar grid round the camera, laid out by its vertex shader from `uCamPos` (`frustumCulled = false`): `mesh` (the sea's, hidden here) and `inland`, the khal, the bil, the ponds and the small khals at their own levels; a level texture over the map (`levelTexture`: r inland water's level, carried `LEVEL_SPREAD` cells out round it over ground no lower than `SPREAD_BELOW` under it; b inland cells) says which water shows where; displaced by the waves (`waveHeightLodGlsl`: each wave fades where the grid is too coarse for it) and by the hero's own waves (`wake.ts`); bed, current (the khal's, from `khal.ts`) and openness from two field textures, a fine one (1 km at 4 m) that follows the camera (`FineField`, refilled a few rows a frame) and a wide one over the map; `update(x, z)` keeps the fine field and sets the mirror's level; water at another level than the mirror's reflects the hazy sky; inland water adds its own murky body; premultiplied and see-through: it outputs only reflection × Fresnel, glitter and foam, and the bed tints itself through `underwater()`; ripples stretched along the current, foam flecks riding it, Kelvin wake, water discarded inside the hero hull. Its sea parts (swell, surf, swash, ships' wakes, the nets' rings) are inert here.

It slows near every event (`LINGER`) and says when an event may be watched (`WATCH`). A day takes about 16 minutes at 1×. It starts on today's date and moon.
- `env/season.ts`: the six Bangla seasons. `KEYS` (one row at each season's middle) sets the land's runoff (`flow`: the khal's current, `uFlowScale`), silt colour, greenness, dryness, blossom, kash, lilies, crop shares, flooded plots, wet soil, cloud, rain, fog, haze, wind (strength, gusts, angle), sunlight and warmth (and the coast's salt work and swell, unused here); `seasonAt` blends them by day, `applySeason` writes the land's uniforms. Also `banglaDate`, `seasonIndex`, `seasonMiddle`, `dayOfYear`, `moonAgeOn`. `env/weather.ts`: showers by the season, cloud, squalls, wetness that dries slowly, lightning; the viewer's choice overrides. `env/wind.ts`: the one wind (`G.uWind`, `G.uGust`), eased toward what the season and the weather ask; `gustAt`/`windAt` (m/s) are the CPU twins of the GLSL `windGust`.
- `env/environment.ts`: light and colour for an hour and moon age. The `LOOKS` table, keyed on sun elevation, sets every colour, the exposure and the mist (dawn adds river mist). Below −4.5° the key light is the moon (by phase and elevation) blended with a faint sky glow from `SKY_GLOW_DIR`; it follows the moon as soon as the moon is up. `aimShadow` decides when the shadow map is redrawn (not while the key is dark). `skyColorAt` paints the timeline.
- `rig/rig.ts`: the boat's journey up the khal and the camera. `boat` is a `BoatBody`; each frame she steers for the point `LOOK_AHEAD` m along the khal from where `khalFrame` puts her (`along`); within `END_REACH` of the journey's end, in the bil, the mist comes down (`fade`, over `LOOP_FADE` s) and she is put back at its start. She opens `OPENING_BELOW_BRIDGE` m below the old bridge. `place(t)` (0..1 of the journey), `progress`. The camera: `follow` (an orbit round a point just ahead of her with drag, wheel, pinch, arrows; idle, it eases back to the chosen `VIEWS` rest view and drifts slowly there; low down it is drawn in along its line until it is over the water (`overWater`), never in among the banks' trees; it is kept above the ground and the water and eases under the bridge's arch, or over its deck, `keepClearOfBridge`) or `free` (`rig/flycam.ts`: WASD/QE, drag to look, kept `EDGE` m inside the world and at most `CEILING` m over the ground; a still placed by `placeFree` is not bounded until the viewer moves it). `rig.watch` is a direction to turn to when idle (a sky event), framed off to one side (`watchYaw`). The camera rests at `OPENING_VIEW` until the viewer picks a view. `main.ts` opens the scene `OPENING_BEFORE_SUNSET` before that day's sunset, and keeps the season's weather off for `OPENING_CLEAR_SECONDS` (`Weather.clearUntil`). Keys reach the rig through `press()` from `main.ts`. `rig/director.ts`: the film tour's engine (scenes joined by dissolves, letterbox, captions; it saves the viewer's world through a `Stage` that `main.ts` provides (`set`, `save`, `restore`, `placeBoat`, `boatPos`, `boatCourse`, `birds`, `sunDir`, `moonDir`, `prepare`) and restores it on hand-back). `rig/tour.ts`: `tourScenes(ghats)`, the 16 scenes along the khal (0 শীতের ভোর, 1 মাঝি, 2 সরিষার মাঠ, 3 গ্রামের ঘাট, 4 বসন্তের রং, 5 কালবৈশাখী, 6 বর্ষার খাল, 7 ডুবো মাঠে আমন, 8 সেতুর নিচে, 9 শাপলা বিল, 10 পুকুরপাড়, 11 কাশবন, 12 হেমন্তের ধান, 13 সূর্যাস্ত, 14 চাঁদ ওঠা, 15 রাতের বিল), each setting its own day, hour or sky event, moon, weather and wind, maybe placing the boat, and posing the camera. The khal is narrow and wooded, so the low shots look along it from its water (`onKhal(s, side, up)`); the fields are one bank (`fieldBank`, the most open plots by the khal, fewest buildings round them) seen in three seasons (2, 7, 12); the sunset finds where the khal runs toward the sun (`facing`) and puts the boat there; the moonrise and the night are out in the open bil, where nothing stands between the boat and the low moon; the kash scene stands where the kash is thickest (`kashStand()`).
- `ui/controls.ts` (+ `controls.css`, imported by it): the bottom dock (play, speed, 24-hour timeline with event markers and the moon's hours, the Bangla date and season, camera mode (boat, free, film), views, moon phase, season, weather, sound, fullscreen, hide UI, help `<dialog>` with the data's credits). It reads the `Clock` each frame and writes the DOM only on change. `ui/bangla.ts`: Bangla digits, spoken time ("সন্ধ্যা ৬:১৪"), tithi, phase and event names. `ui/hud.ts`: loader, title, hint, film captions, event notices.
- `audio/soundscape.ts` (Web Audio synthesis: the khal's water murmuring (a little more at night), the lapping, wind, rain, the paddle's splash each stroke (`splash(power)`), a magpie-robin's phrases by day, crickets by night, thunder; `update(dt, night, wind, rain)`), `main.ts` (bootstrap, loader steps, adaptive resolution and the quality tier, the stage for the film, every keyboard shortcut in one `keydown`, sky-event watching, the season, weather and moon picks eased and held for a session, the `?debug` hooks).

## Invariants and gotchas (each one cost a bug)

- Never toggle `visible` on a light. Changing the light count recompiles every material (a 60 ms+ hitch at sunset). Set the intensity to 0 instead.
- Shadow maps use `renderer.shadowMap.autoUpdate = false`. Redraw only when `Environment.aimShadow()` returns true; it checks whether the focus moved or the key light turned.
- Post passes sanitize NaN/Inf with comparisons, not `isnan()`. ANGLE/D3D may optimise `isnan()` away. A single bad pixel otherwise smears into black squares through the bloom chain. Birds once produced such a pixel, and the root cause is still unknown (see `HANDOFF.md`).
- Alpha-tested cards lose thin strokes in their mipmaps. Keep a dense core in the texture, as the kash plume does.
- The kash's plumes are hidden out of season by brightness alone (`seasonal: 'kash'` in `patch.ts`): the plume must stay the only bright part of `kashTexture`, and the test runs before the season's tint. A test on saturation as well missed the plumes' warm white once the vertex colour and the dry months' tint touched it, and they stood white all year.
- Foliage cards use `foliage: true`, which keeps the vertex normal on back faces, so a crown shades as one soft volume.
- The water discards fragments inside the hero hull using `hullHalfBeamAtWater`, which is generated from `HERO_HULL`. Edit the hull only in `hull.ts`, and the GLSL follows.
- Fog is split between passes. The mirror pass fogs only water → object (the `uReflPass` branch of `applyAtmosphere`), and the water shader fogs camera → water.
- `tsconfig` has `erasableSyntaxOnly`: no constructor parameter properties, enums or namespaces. It also has `noUnusedLocals`/`noUnusedParameters`.
- Watch for aliasing of shared temporary vectors. The boatman's arm IK broke because an output vector was also read as an input. `khalFrame` returns one reused object: copy what you need before calling it again.
- Cameras and the film follow `boat.course` (smoothed); `boat.heading` wags about a degree with each stroke and would shake them.
- The hero group's rotation is `(rotX, heading, rotZ, 'YXZ')`: rotX positive is bow down, rotZ positive is starboard up. Local hull height is `heave + x·rotZ − z·rotX`.
- Boat and boatman form a loop: `hero.crew` (where his weight is) feeds `rig.update` one frame late; `hero.update` reads the body to pose him.
- The boatman's hands are placed on the paddle before his arms are solved (`Human.holdBar` uses last frame's elbow), and the arm IK then targets the wrist that leaves; `setHand` before `setArm` (the forearm's twist reads the hand). Don't solve the arm first: the hand would float off the paddle. `hero.ts` passes each hold as a point on the bar plus the bar's direction toward the thumb.
- A `Human`'s bones are flat (all children of its `group`, no rotation in the bind): a bone's pose is where its head goes and its turn from the bind, and positions are in the owner's frame (the boat's, the girl's). Its meshes are skinned by dual quaternions (`DQ_BASE` etc. in `human.ts`, from `set`), not three's matrices: every material on a `Human` goes through `dualQuaternions` (the constructor does it for its own and the garments passed in), and nothing moves until `commit()`. Its meshes' bounding sphere is carried with the hips in `setTrunk`; one left at the bind culled the boatman whenever the boat's middle was off screen.
- The IK must use the body's own lengths (`body.dims`): the bones are rigid, so an elbow or knee the IK puts elsewhere than the bind's length stretches the skin. Things laid on a person (the gamchha's and orna's pins, the plait's root, the hat) are bind points carried by a bone (`onChest`, `onHead`, a child of `bone(name)`).
- A fitted garment (`vest`, `kameez`, `salwar`) is a shell over the skin built in `scripts/humans/build.mjs`: its edge is the zero of a smooth field (ellipsoids for necks and armholes, planes for hems, the arm weights for sleeves; never a per-vertex yes/no, which cut staircases along the quads), carried in the vertex colour's alpha and cut by `alphaTest` (`Human` sets it, with alpha to coverage); the skin well inside it is left out of the body, so it cannot poke through. The shell is smoothed (cloth bridges small shapes) and its front, and with `back` its back, hangs straight from the fullest point above. A vest draped that way over a man's chest stayed behind as the pectorals rose with the arms and read as a bust: the sando is not draped.
- Loose cloth on a person (lungi, skirt, gamchha, orna) is sized from the `sections` the build measures, not constants: the thigh's profile is its widest reach, so rings round it use about 0.8 of it.
- The paddle stroke lives only in `paddle.ts`: the blade's path, the boatman's body, the boat's thrust and the water's rings all read it, so change the stroke there and everything stays in step. `MEAN_THRUST` must match the drag at `CRUISE` (`DRAG` in `boatPhysics.ts`).
- The journey is the khal's middle line (`khalAt(s)`, `s` in metres along it): the boat steers for a point `LOOK_AHEAD` m further along it from her own `s`, so she follows its bends wherever they run; anything laid along the khal is placed by `s`, `side` (+1 left going up) and the distance out from the middle (`khalHalfWidth(s)` is the water's edge; the mud bank runs `BANK` m beyond it to the levee). The film and the stills give the boat's place as a share of `JOURNEY.length`.
- The ground near the khal is `baseGround` (the map let down by `corridorDrop` so one water level serves the khal and the bil): compare heights there with `terrainHeight`/`baseGround`, never with `heightAt` (the map's own, up to ≈4 m higher). Ponds' levels and the roads' crests are let down with it.
- A pond's water is the map's measured surface, and the map's ground beside it is often a little lower: `pondBank` raises a bank (পাড়) round every pond, and `levelTexture` carries a water's level outward only over ground no lower than `SPREAD_BELOW` under it. Without either, ponds' water stood in the air over the fields beside them like white plates.
- OSM draws roads right beside the canal; the khal is drawn wider than at 1:3, so they would stand in it: `besideTheKhal` (roads.ts) moves a road's run beside the khal onto the bank it came in on, and only a short run from one bank to the other is a crossing (a deck). Judging crossings by the line changing sides made a road that wandered across the khal's middle line into a walled deck running along the khal.
- Grass, flowers and crop patches ask `onRoad` (and the bridge's `bridgePath`) for each plant: the ground lattice is too coarse to see a 1.2 m footpath. The terrain's plots keep off the roads and the khal's bank and levee strip (`khalBank`); the meadow keeps off the ghats.
- Placements (huts, haystacks, trees) must check `nearBackwater` (through `onDryLand` in vegetation) or they stand in a khal, a pond or the bil, and `onRoad` or they stand on a road. Water shows only where `levelTexture` (water.ts) puts it: inland water over inland cells and what it adds (the bil, and the khal and every small khal through `eachWaterwayPoint`). A new carved water must be painted there too. Small khals are narrower than the field's 4 m texels, so the inland water is discarded only where the field's bed is well above its level; the depth test hides it under the banks.
- What stands at the khal's edge reads `khalFrame`/`khalHalfWidth` (the water's edge), `khalBank` (its mud slope and levee strip) and `shoreLevel` (the level of the water a bank belongs to; `waterLevelAt` is −∞ on the dry bank).
- Floating plants keep to the slack water along the khal's banks (`EDGE` in `drift.ts`) and the bil's lilies off the boat's way (`routeGap`): the khal is the boats' way and stays open.
- Seen from above the water, the haze ends at its surface (`applyAtmosphere` cuts the ray there): the height fog's density grows below sea level, and a deep bed used to come out brighter than a shallow one.
- Past the baked map's edges `cellAt` hands back the edge cells, which run on as stripes to the horizon: `applyAtmosphere` fades the land beyond the edges into the haze (`EDGE_HAZE`, from `uLevelMap`), and the free camera stays `EDGE` m inside them and below `CEILING`.
- Wave slopes evaluated per pixel must fade by the pixel's footprint (`waveSlopeLod` with `fwidth`): short waves sampled too coarsely alias into rings round the eye on open water.
- Lines drawn with a fixed colour floor glow against dark ground (power lines' wires once did, at night and in shadow): light anything a pixel wide from the key light and the sky, and blend it by the share of the pixel it would really cover.
- `taperedTube` in `render/builder.ts` winds its triangles inward (known, not yet fixed project-wide; see `HANDOFF.md`). New single-sided geometry should use outward winding.
- Cloth vertex colours carry shading, so cloth materials need `vertexColors: true`; anything added with them must have a colour attribute (build it with `MeshBuilder`), or it renders black.
- Waves that move things must come from `waves.ts` (`waveHeight`/`waveSlope`, `hullWaves` for hulls, and the GLSL twins in shaders), so floating things ride what the eye sees. The sea state is set once a frame by `updateSea()` from the wind, gusts and rain (in `main.ts`'s `updateSky`); each wave's amplitude reaches the shaders as `G.uWaveA/B/C`. Hulls use `hullWaves` (the waves averaged over the waterline), not point samples: a few samples let short, steep chop roll the boat ±25°.
- A cut in the film takes two frames: the director's update runs before the frame is drawn, so it holds one frame (`Pipeline` `hold`) and opens the next scene on the following frame, which dissolves from the held one. The held texture must be unbound while it is being written (a feedback loop otherwise silently cancels the draw).
- Vegetation's cost is draw calls (species shapes × slabs × mirror) and alpha-tested overdraw as much as triangles: before adding a species, shape or density, check `__river.stats('vegetation')` (instances and triangles per shape) and the frame time.
- Debug-only code stays behind `?debug`. The debug `shot()` places the boat after the water level has settled; `mats()`/`matsAt()` and `trace()` are there for checking the hyacinth and the journey (`trace` samples `along`, the bed's depth, the boatman's reach and her speed); `people()` (the boatman's and the girl's heads, for framing close-ups) and `hide(name)`/`show(name)`. `shot()` freezes the frame loop: call `resume()` before holding keys (dispatched `KeyboardEvent`s reach the real handlers). A long `trace()` leaves the page idle long enough to start the film: send a key straight after it. A probe's `import('/src/…')` gets its own module instances: their functions are right but `G` there is not the page's (read the shader clock as `__river.info().time`).
- All the water is inland: the khal and the bil at `KHAL_LEVEL`, the ponds and small khals at their own levels (`waterLevelAt`, −∞ on dry ground; `waterSurface(xz)` in a shader). `G.uWaterLevel` and `uTideHigh` are held at `KHAL_LEVEL` only for what still reads them (`main.ts`); never compare a height with `uWaterLevel` alone. Floating bodies with a heave spring move with the level under them (`BoatBody`).
- Anything the wind moves takes it from `windAt`/`gustAt` (CPU) or `windGust` (GLSL), so trees, water, cloth, boats and plants agree.
- Things that move must not cast shadows (the shadow map is not redrawn every frame, so their shadows would stay behind): the hero boat.
- Camera-facing quads built in a vertex shader (the rain) must be `DoubleSide`: which face shows depends on the slant. The rain was invisible for a whole task because of this.
- Anything that reads `Cover.zone` must allow for its sign (paddy > 0): the meadow uses `1 − |zone|`.
- The water is see-through: every material that can sit below the water must go through `patchMaterial`, or it will show through the khal untinted.
- The ground blend samples only the two strongest layers, with the layer index as a texture coordinate. Arrays indexed in loops (`w[i]`, `cs[i]`) took frames from 16.7 ms to 26–32 ms in headless Chrome (ANGLE/Direct3D).
- The meadow builds only a few chunks a frame; stills call `meadow.update(camera.position, true)` to build them all.
- The orbit camera always looks at the boat, so it cannot look up at a high moon; stills of a high moon need `shot({ free, face })`.
- `ClothTube.update` lays each ring's depth (`b`) on once, as given. `girl.ts` once passed half and her kameez came out flat from the side; it passes the full depth now. Keep cloth off the body with a `collide` where limbs pass through it.
- Sky event times come from `Clock.events()`; compare events by `clock.watching(ev)`, not identity (the cache is rebuilt each day and on a moon change).
- Season, weather and moon picks are eased (the year turns over `YEAR_TURN_SECONDS` through `shownDay()`, the moon through `shownMoon()`), and each holds for `SESSION_MS` before `backToToday()`; read the shown day and moon, not the clock's, for anything drawn.
- The paddy plots have a CPU twin of their shader layout (`glsl.ts` ↔ `crops.ts`): change both together.
- The quality tier (`setQuality` in `main.ts`) only scales what is already there: cull distances (`setCullReach`), the mirror's resolution (`Pipeline.setReflectionScale`), the shadow map (`Environment.setShadowMapSize`, which lets the old map go so a new size takes), the grass's reach (`Meadow.setReach`). Anything new that is costly far off should register with `cullBeyond` so the tier reaches it.
- The data's credits come from `manifest.json` `attribution` (written by `bake.mjs`); the help sheet lists them (`#sources`, `controls.ts`) and the README repeats them. A new data source must add its credit there. FABDEM is CC BY-NC-SA: the site stays non-commercial.
- The water mesh is laid round the camera in its vertex shader; anything that floats on it must use `waves.ts` (and `wake.ts` near the hero) with the same shelter, or it will sit off the surface.
- Distance culling (`render/cull.ts`) owns `visible` on the meshes registered with `cullBeyond`, and their bounding spheres are taken once, at build: register only things that stay put, and never set `visible` on those meshes yourself.
- Places come from `scripts/geo/places.mjs` (baked to `places.json`, with sources): the khal is the OSM canal nearest `arch-bridge`, the bil is at `shapla-bil`; `BRIDGE` stands where the khal passes the bridge's place. Move a place there and rebake; what is laid out round it follows.
- Inland water's level is the median of the DEM's surface over the water round it (±2 cells), and water the DEM puts on a slope is ground (`INLAND_SLOPE`); otherwise ponds become tilted sheets.
- The map is baked, not drawn: change ground, water or places in `scripts/geo/` and rerun `bake.mjs` (then `check.mjs`); never hand-edit `public/geo/`.
- `applyAtmosphere` integrates the fog and mist as a difference of the ray's two ends' exponentials. The product form (`exp(-y0 f) · (1 − exp(−dist k)) / k`) is 0 × ∞ = NaN in fp32 once the eye is a few hundred metres up, and the whole frame goes dark.

## Conventions

- TypeScript strict. Keep modules small and focused, put the reason in comments, and match the surrounding style.
- Keep it procedural apart from the six CC0 textures in `public/textures/` and the people's MakeHuman (CC0) data in `public/humans/`; ask before adding any other asset. External requests: those textures (same origin) and the Noto Serif Bengali Google Font, with system fallbacks.
- UI copy and captions are in Bangla. The people are realistic but generic (MakeHuman's bodies shaped by its macro sliders): no likeness of any real person.
- Keep the people's assets to MakeHuman's CC0 data through `scripts/humans/`; a new character or garment goes in `config.mjs` and `build.mjs`, then the three commands.
- Scope (this branch): the khal and the bil only, the journey, the camera and the film. Before adding anything, ask whether it would really be there on a village khal, and whether it makes the river journey better.
