# নদী — a real-time 3D Bengal river at golden hour, down the Moheshkhali channel

Standalone site: Vite 8 + TypeScript 7 + three r186. Almost everything (terrain, trees, grass, huts, boats, most textures, sound) is generated in code. The one exception, chosen by the user in the second session: six CC0 Poly Haven textures (colour + normal, 1K WebP, ≈4.5 MB) in `public/textures/` for the ground layers and the bark. There are no model or audio files.

## Commands

- `npm run dev` serves on http://localhost:5173. `npm run build` writes `dist/` (hashed files in `dist/bundle/`). `npm run typecheck` runs `tsc --noEmit`. `npm run preview` serves `dist/`.
- Visual check (only when asked): with the dev server running, `node scripts/headless/shoot.mjs <outDir> --shots '<json>'`. It saves PNGs and prints draw calls, triangles, frame ms and every page console message. It needs Chrome (set `CHROME_PATH` if Chrome is not at the default Windows path). The shot format and the debug API are documented in `HANDOFF.md`.
- The build warns that the bundle is over 500 kB. That is expected: three.js is a large share of it (the whole bundle is ≈896 kB, ≈265 kB gzipped).

## Architecture (`src/`)

- `core/globals.ts`: `G`, the uniforms every scene shader shares (time, pass camera position, sun, key light, glow, fog, mist, night, wind and gust phase, water level, flow scale, the season's `uSeasonA`/`uSeasonB`/`uCrops`, wet, rain, overcast). Materials hold references to these objects, so mutate `.value` and never replace an object. Layers: `LAYER_WATER = 1`, `LAYER_NO_REFLECT = 2`. Both are drawn by the main camera only; the mirror camera sees layer 0.
- `render/glsl.ts`: `GLSL_COMMON` (uniform declarations, hash/noise/fbm, `windGust`, `plotFrame`/`PLOT_SIZE`/`cropShares` (the paddy plots and what each holds now), `hazeColor`, `applyAtmosphere`). Include it exactly once per shader. Also `CROP_REACH` (how far the 3D crops stand; inside it the terrain paints the ground under them).
- `render/patch.ts`: `patchMaterial(material, options)` injects the shared atmosphere into built-in materials, plus optional wind sway, grime, translucency, terrain paddy plots, riverbed caustics, foliage normals, and `splat` (the photo ground layers), through `onBeforeCompile`; also `seasonal: 'leaf' | 'tree' | 'kash'` (the season's tint, spring blossom on a share of the trees, kash plumes only in autumn) and `vertex` hooks. The terrain's `aZone` is signed: paddy plots > 0, salt pans < 0 (the salt works' layout has a JS twin, `LAYOUT`/`blockKind` in `world/coast.ts`; the paddy plots' one is in `world/crops.ts`); still water standing on the ground mirrors the sky (`atmBrine`). It sets `material.defines` and `customProgramCacheKey`; any new option must change that key. Its fog step also calls `underwater()` (in `glsl.ts`): anything below the water level fades into `G.uWaterBody` with the length of water it is seen through.
- `render/surfaces.ts`: loads the photo textures (`GROUND_LAYERS` grass, dirt, mud, sand, pebbles as two `DataArrayTexture`s, colour and normal, plus each layer's mean colour; and the bark). A set that fails to load is `null`, and the scene falls back to its painted look.
- `render/pipeline.ts`: `Pipeline` runs, in order: mirror pass (half-res `PlanarReflection`, `uReflPass = 1`, `uCamPos` = mirror camera) → scene (HalfFloat, MSAA ×4) → bright pass → 5-level bloom → radial light shafts → composite (ACES, grade, vignette, grain, chromatic fringe, fades, letterbox) straight to the canvas.
- `render/reflection.ts`: Reflector-style mirror camera for the plane at `G.uWaterLevel`, with an oblique near plane.
- `render/builder.ts`: `MeshBuilder` (merges procedural parts into one geometry), `rgb()` (sRGB hex → linear triple), `taperedTube`, `card`. `render/instancing.ts`: `instanced()` splits placements into slabs of 600 m along z by 1300 m across x (`chunk`, `across`) so off-screen slabs are culled. `render/cull.ts`: `cullBeyond(root, far)` records each mesh's world bounding sphere once; `updateCulling(eye)` (each frame, before drawing) leaves meshes undrawn beyond `far` (`main.ts`: salt works 1100 m, small landmarks 1500 m, vegetation 1900 m). `render/textures.ts`: every texture is painted on a canvas and turned into a `DataTexture`. Rows are flipped so v = 1 is the canvas top, and `bleed` fills transparent texels with the average colour.
- `world/terrain.ts` also exports `groundAt(x, z)` / `Cover`: painted colour, field zone (signed, see above) and the photo-layer weights at a point. The terrain's `aSplat` attribute and the meadow's grass density both come from it.
- `world/crops.ts`: 3D crops in the paddy plots round the camera (out to `CROP_REACH`): rice in rows (seedling, growing, ripe with nodding panicles, stubble) and winter mustard in flower, in three LODs (real plants < 20 m, row cards + canopy < 44 m, canopy only beyond). Each patch works out its plot's crop in the vertex shader from `plotFrame`/`cropShares`, so the year turning regrows the fields with nothing rebuilt; rice and mustard meshes share instance buffers (the absent one is collapsed below ground); the wind bends each plant from its foot (`sway`). Patches are laid per plot between its bunds.
- `world/meadow.ts`: dense blade grass, wild flowers and waterline pebbles round the camera, built in 12 m chunks (two levels of detail, a few chunks per frame, from a 1.5 m height lattice) into one `InstancedMesh` per layer. `world/drift.ts`: water hyacinth as position-based dynamics: 720 plants (each its own body, tied to neighbours in mats by tension-only root links that snap when overstretched) and 160 leaves, dragged toward the local current and a little downwind, colliding through a spatial hash, pushed out of the hero hull and by the oar blade, nudged off the banks, bobbing and tilting on the shared waves; recycled round the viewer. It also meets `DriftObstacles` from `Fishing.obstacles()`: the anchored boats' hulls, the A-frames' poles, and the simulated nets (a wall where the netting crosses the surface; a rising net scoops up what floats over it and holds it until it goes back in). `world/ponds.ts`: lily pads and flowers on the village ponds and the small wetlands, and the wetlands' reeds and floating hyacinth.
- `world/geography.ts` is the single source of truth for land and water: `riverX`, `halfWidth` and `riverDepth` (from the `REACHES` table: broad shallow reaches round the chars, deep narrows between them), `streamSpeed` (faster through the narrows), `edgeHalfWidth`, `CHARS` and `SHOALS` (submerged sandbars), `KHALS` (canals off the river), `BIL` (the shapla bil behind the left bank: `bilReach`, `bilDepth`, `bilMarsh`) and `BIL_ROUTE` (the boat's detour through it, with channels cut through the bank), `VILLAGES`/`VILLAGE_CENTERS`, `PONDS`, `WETLANDS` (small seasonal wetlands placed by `findWetlands`, irregular through `wetlandReach`), `coastal(z)` and `saltPan(x, z)` (downstream of `COAST_Z0` the river opens into an estuary between low salt flats), `landLevel`, `charHeight`, `terrainHeight`, `bankInfo`, `nearBackwater` (for placements), `riverLane` (the river's own lane; traffic keeps to it) and `boatLane` (the hero's, including the detour), `BRIDGE` (the old bridge over the bil's inlet channel, with `bridgeFrame`/`bridgeToWorld`, `bridgeSoffit`, `bridgeDeck`, `bridgePath`, `onBridge`), and the world (z 460 → −4300) and journey (320 → −3920) extents. The river flows toward −z, and the boat travels from `JOURNEY_Z0` to `JOURNEY_Z1`. The world is the Moheshkhali channel at about 1:5 along its length: the right bank (u > 0, west) is Moheshkhali island, with its own ground (`islandLevel`: `ISLAND`, `islandCoast`/`islandNorthCoast`/`islandWestCoast`, beaches and the sea floor beyond), its hills (`hillHeight` along `hillCrestU`, 50–85 m; `ADINATH`, the temple's flat-topped knoll), the Kohelia (`KOHELIA`, private) down its low west, and Sonadia (`SONADIA`) off its south end; the left bank is the mainland, with far hills (`mainlandHills`, falling away before the world's edges) and Cox's Bazar (`COXS_BAZAR`) at the mouth. Landmark sites: `GORAKGHATA` (jetty, town strip, stair, pagoda), `SHUTKI_YARDS`; `onLandmark(x, z, margin)` and `onTempleHill` keep placements off them. Cover helpers for painting and planting: `forestCover`, `sandCover`, `mangroveZone`. `saltPan` also covers the island's tract (south-west, and beyond the Kohelia) and ends in `worked()`, a sharp step.
- `world/`: `terrain.ts` (grid laid out across the river from its centre line, 8 chunks, paddy mask in `aZone`, layer weights in `aSplat`), `water.ts` (a polar grid round the camera, laid out by its vertex shader from `uCamPos` (`frustumCulled = false`), displaced by the waves (`waveHeightLodGlsl`: each wave fades where the grid is too coarse for it) and by the hero's own waves (`wake.ts`), clamped to `WATER_BOUNDS`; ## Invariants and gotchas (each one cost a bug)

- Never toggle `visible` on a light. Changing the light count recompiles every material (a 60 ms+ hitch at sunset). Set the intensity to 0 instead.
- Shadow maps use `renderer.shadowMap.autoUpdate = false`. Redraw only when `Environment.aimShadow()` returns true; it checks whether the focus moved or the key light turned.
- Post passes sanitize NaN/Inf with comparisons, not `isnan()`. ANGLE/D3D may optimise `isnan()` away. A single bad pixel otherwise smears into black squares through the bloom chain. Birds once produced such a pixel, and the root cause is still unknown (see `HANDOFF.md`).
- Alpha-tested cards lose thin strokes in their mipmaps. Keep a dense core in the texture, as the kash plume does.
- Foliage cards use `foliage: true`, which keeps the vertex normal on back faces, so a crown shades as one soft volume.
- The water discards fragments inside the hero hull using `hullHalfBeamAtWater`, which is generated from `HERO_HULL`. Edit the hull only in `hull.ts`, and the GLSL follows.
- Fog is split between passes. The mirror pass fogs only water → object (the `uReflPass` branch of `applyAtmosphere`), and the water shader fogs camera → water.
- `tsconfig` has `erasableSyntaxOnly`: no constructor parameter properties, enums or namespaces. It also has `noUnusedLocals`/`noUnusedParameters`.
- Watch for aliasing of shared temporary vectors. The boatman's arm IK broke because an output vector was also read as an input.
- Cameras and the film follow `boat.course` (smoothed); `boat.heading` wags about a degree with each stroke and would shake them.
- The hero group's rotation is `(rotX, heading, rotZ, 'YXZ')`: rotX positive is bow down, rotZ positive is starboard up. Local hull height is `heave + x·rotZ − z·rotX`.
- Boat and boatman form a loop: `hero.crew` (where his weight is) feeds `rig.update` one frame late; `hero.update` reads the body to pose him.
- The boatman's hands are placed on the paddle before his arms are solved (`placeHand` uses last frame's elbow), and the arm IK then targets the wrist that leaves. Don't solve the arm first: the hand would float off the paddle. `hero.ts` passes each hold as a point on the bar plus the bar's direction toward the thumb.
- The paddle stroke lives only in `paddle.ts`: the blade's path, the boatman's body, the boat's thrust and the water's rings all read it, so change the stroke there and everything stays in step.
- The boat's route must keep moving toward −z (steering looks a fixed distance ahead in z). Its detour through the bil is water all the way only because `BIL_ROUTE` carves channels where it crosses land; move a route point and the channel moves with it, but keep it clear of chars and villages. River traffic uses `riverLane`, never `boatLane`.
- Placements (huts, haystacks, trees) must check `nearBackwater` (through `onDryLand` in vegetation) or they stand in a khal or the bil. Anything carved below the water level shows water automatically (the water plane is flat and everywhere).
- `taperedTube` in `render/builder.ts` winds its triangles inward (known, not yet fixed project-wide; see `HANDOFF.md`). New single-sided geometry should use outward winding, as `anatomy.ts` does.
- Cloth vertex colours carry shading, so cloth materials need `vertexColors: true`; anything added with them must have a colour attribute (build it with `MeshBuilder`), or it renders black.
- Waves that move things must come from `waves.ts` (`waveHeight`/`waveSlope`, `hullWaves` for hulls, and the GLSL twins in shaders), so floating things ride what the eye sees. The sea state is set once a frame by `updateSea()` from the wind, gusts and rain (in `main.ts`'s `updateSky`); each wave's amplitude reaches the shaders as `G.uWaveA/B/C`. Hulls use `hullWaves` (the waves averaged over the waterline), not point samples: a few samples let short, steep chop roll the boat ±25°.
- A cut in the film takes two frames: the director's update runs before the frame is drawn, so it holds one frame (`Pipeline` `hold`) and opens the next scene on the following frame, which dissolves from the held one. The held texture must be unbound while it is being written (a feedback loop otherwise silently cancels the draw).
- Debug-only code stays behind `?debug`. The debug `shot()` places the boat after the water level has settled; `nets()`, `wetlands()`, `matsAt()`, `caught()` and `trace()` are there for checking the fishing, the hyacinth, the wetlands and the route (see `HANDOFF.md`).
- The water's surface is `G.uWaterLevel` (it moves with the seasons), never y = 0. Anything that floats, wades or is clamped above the water reads it, on the CPU or in a vertex hook.
- Anything the wind moves takes it from `windAt`/`gustAt` (CPU) or `windGust` (GLSL), so trees, water, cloth, boats, nets and plants agree.
- Things that move must not cast shadows (the shadow map is not redrawn every frame, so their shadows would stay behind): the hero boat, the fishing boats, the levers, arms and nets.
- Camera-facing quads built in a vertex shader (the rain) must be `DoubleSide`: which face shows depends on the slant. The rain was invisible for a whole task because of this.
- `LiftNet` skips its cloth step for steps under 4 ms: the strings' corrections over a tiny step become huge forces on the lever.
- Anything that reads `Cover.zone` must allow for its sign (paddy > 0, salt pans < 0): the meadow uses `1 − |zone|`.
- Floating plants in the small wetlands are set only over beds below the driest season's water (−0.5 m), or they would float over dry ground.
- The water is see-through: every material that can sit below the water must go through `patchMaterial`, or it will show through the river untinted.
- The ground blend samples only the two strongest layers, with the layer index as a texture coordinate. Arrays indexed in loops (`w[i]`, `cs[i]`) took frames from 16.7 ms to 26–32 ms in headless Chrome (ANGLE/Direct3D).
- The meadow builds only a few chunks a frame; stills call `meadow.update(camera.position, true)` to build them all.
- The orbit camera always looks at the boat, so it cannot look up at a high moon; stills of a high moon need `shot({ free, face })`.
- `ClothTube.update` adds each ring's depth offset (`az`) twice (a known slip, left as is at the user's wish so the boatman and fishermen keep their look). New tubes must pass half the depth they want (as `girl.ts` does) and keep cloth off the body with their own `collide`.
- Sky event times come from `Clock.events()`; compare events by `clock.watching(ev)`, not identity (the cache is rebuilt each day and on a moon change).
- Season, weather and moon picks are eased (the year turns over `YEAR_TURN_SECONDS` through `shownDay()`, the moon through `shownMoon()`), and each holds for `SESSION_MS` before `backToToday()`; read the shown day and moon, not the clock's, for anything drawn.
- Two layouts have CPU twins of a shader: the paddy plots (`glsl.ts` ↔ `crops.ts`) and the salt works (`patch.ts` ↔ `coast.ts` `LAYOUT`/`blockKind`). Change both together.
- The water mesh is laid round the camera in its vertex shader; anything that floats on it must use `waves.ts` (and `wake.ts` near the hero) with the same shelter, or it will sit off the surface.
- Distance culling (`render/cull.ts`) owns `visible` on the meshes registered with `cullBeyond`, and their bounding spheres are taken once, at build: register only things that stay put, and never set `visible` on those meshes yourself.
- Landmark positions live in `geography.ts` (`GORAKGHATA`, `ADINATH`, `COXS_BAZAR`, `SHUTKI_YARDS`) and `moheshkhali.ts` builds from them; huts, trees, grass and salt pans keep off them through `onLandmark` (trees also through `onTempleHill`, which keeps the temple visible from the channel). Move a landmark there and its clearing follows.
- The terrain shader mixes the salt pans in by `saltPan`'s value, so a long fade draws faint pans through the grass: keep its edges short (it ends in `worked()`).
- On the island (u > 0) the ground comes from `islandLevel`; the coastal lowering in `landLevel` applies to the mainland only. Hills there must leave the side +1 khals their valleys (`hillHeight` scales down within 80 m of them).

## Conventions

- TypeScript strict. Keep modules small and focused, put the reason in comments, and match the surrounding style.
- Keep it procedural apart from the six CC0 textures in `public/textures/`; ask before adding any other asset. External requests: those textures (same origin) and the Noto Serif Bengali Google Font, with system fallbacks.
- UI copy and captions are in Bangla. No realistic faces or likenesses of real people: the boatmen are faceless and stylised.
